About Study Spot

Study Spot helps students and remote workers discover, evaluate, book and review study spaces — and offers tools to improve concentration while they're there.

FAQ

Do I need an account to book?

Yes — bookings are tied to your account so you can manage and cancel them. Browsing and the companion timer work as a guest.

How does availability work?

Each venue has a capacity per 2-hour slot. Slots show how many seats remain and close automatically when full.

What happens after I submit a reservation?

Instant-approval venues confirm immediately. Others send a request to the venue and you'll see a pending status until it's approved.

Can I save favorites as a guest?

No — favorites require an account so they sync across devices.

How do I report a review?

Use the flag icon on any review. Reports go to moderators.

Privacy policy

We store your name, email and a hashed password. Booking, review and study-session data are linked to your account only. You can hide your name from public reviews at any time in account settings. We never sell personal data.

Terms of use

Reviews must reflect a genuine visit. Spam, harassment and fake bookings are removed and may lead to account suspension. Venue information is community-maintained and may change without notice.

Booking & cancellation policy

  • Free cancellation up to 2 hours before the session start.
  • No-shows may be recorded and can limit future bookings.
  • Paid venues are refunded in full when cancelled within policy.
  • Pending requests can be cancelled at any time before approval.
